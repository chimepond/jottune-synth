# JotTune Synth for macOS

The Records view imports complete TL files into a retained local collection
and offers playback, seek, listening profiles, meters, spectrum and spatial
inspection. The development build can also browse its configured source library. The native bridge uses the engine's reader and renderer.

The Instrument view imports patches and recipes, previews a note, edits patch
statements, plays from the keyboard or MIDI, and saves patches and takes.
Inactive controls explain the applicable engine conditions. Patch text remains
the complete editable representation; unsupported recipe exports retain their
unrepresentable statements as pending comments.

The Features view shows parameter and generator catalogs produced by the same
native build. See the instrument guide for synthesis, effects, spatial modes,
units and authoring examples. No timeline editing is provided.
