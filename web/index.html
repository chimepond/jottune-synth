Keep loaded version preserves the saved version named above. Preparing a profile resets playback; press Play when ready.

What do the profiles change?

Original is the composer’s original version: it may be mono, stereo, moving or binaural. Saved Keys versions usually use ±30°: low notes left, middle C ahead, high notes right. Keys mirror reverses left and right, like facing the piano from the audience.

The number is the angle on each side of the listener. ±30° spans 60° in total; ±60° spans 120°. Keys places note attacks on a one-metre front arc, from E1 at the left edge to G♯6 at the right; pitches outside that range stay at the edges. Wider angles increase separation. ±90° reaches the sides.

Computed Keys replaces authored spatial motion with pitch-based ordinary stereo; it does not add binaural HRTF rendering. Notes, timing and effects remain; carrier levels are adjusted together toward the existing peak target. Headphones make the comparison easiest. Prepare profile creates new audio silently; press Play to listen.

Each change starts from the loaded source, so widths do not accumulate. Choose Keep loaded version and Prepare profile to restore it. Use Save song (.tl) to keep the computed version as one complete song. Nothing is uploaded or added to the catalog.

Computing a profile takes several renders and can be slower than loading a saved version, especially on a phone. Cancel stops the work.

Optional recovery for quiet songs: preserves relative carrier levels and still refuses clipping or silence. The result is labelled below target rather than normalized.

A .tl song file reopens in this instrument; .wav is rendered audio.

Library songs use a compact link; local edits travel inside a portable link. If a QR is too large, share the TL file. Portable links depend on the selected renderer remaining available.

Timeline source

Editing stops playback and keeps the selected renderer. Listen renders the current text and starts audio. Save draft text retains unfinished or refused text locally, without claiming verification.

For an intentional new version, keep the original saved TL and edit a separate draft without its old hash assertion. Never remove a hash to conceal an unexplained mismatch. After a valid draft render, Verify saved render replays its canonical output and checks the new saved hash. Keep your editable source too: canonical output can reorder headers and add defaults.
